# Grant Application & Weekly Update Rules

## Competing chain separation

When submitting a grant application or weekly update for a specific chain/program,
do NOT mention competing chains or their programs:

- **Monad / DeltaV updates & grant apps** — do not mention Avalanche, Avalanche Team1,
  or any Avalanche-specific programs.
- **Avalanche / Team1 grant apps** — do not mention Monad, DeltaV, IRMarket,
  or any Monad-specific programs.

Keep each submission scoped to its own chain ecosystem only.
